# Code of conduct

Mixtape follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be welcoming, kind and patient. Assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and respect people of every background and level of experience. Harassment, insults, discrimination, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project's spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block people who don't follow this code.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the email address on their GitHub profile, or via a private security report if no address is listed. Reports are handled confidentially.
